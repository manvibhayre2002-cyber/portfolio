    How the money works

    This is the part I was stuck on the longest: if Rohan puts money down for seats that aren't theirs, how does that money find its way back? I landed on one rule that keeps it simple.

    The reserve is a guarantee, not a payment for a friend.

    It only holds the seat until the friend pays. The moment they do, Rohan's reserve for that seat comes back on its own, to the original payment method, or instantly as BookMyShow credit if Rohan prefers. And if Rohan decides to cover that friend after all, the reserve simply counts towards the price, so they only pay the rest. Every rupee always has one clear owner.

    How long should friends get to pay?

    Too short and friends miss the link; too long and the cinema is stuck with seats it can't sell. Before picking numbers, I looked at how airlines, railways and BookMyShow itself already handle holds and cancellations.

    IndiGo 6E Fare Hold

    You can hold a fare for 48 or 72 hrs for a small, non-refundable fee (from ₹99). Pay within the hold or it cancels itself. An older rule didn't allow holds within 48 hrs of travel.

    What I took from it

    A hold needs some money behind it, a hard deadline, and shouldn't be allowed too close to departure.

    IRCTC

    Cancelling 12 to 4 hrs before departure costs 50%. Once the chart is prepared (4–8 hrs before, sources differ after the 2025 changes), there's no refund at all.

    What I took from it

    There's a "chart" moment when unclaimed seats freeze and get reallocated, and fees rise as departure gets closer.

    BookMyShow today

    You can cancel up to 20 min before the show. It costs 25% of the base price if it's more than 2 hrs away, 50% after that. Split bookings can't be cancelled.

    What I took from it

    Cinemas already accept losing a seat for 25% compensation when it's released 2+ hrs before the show.

    Movies are different from flights and trains, though. People plan a film days ahead, not weeks; a ₹300 ticket doesn't justify a 72-hour hold; and cinemas keep selling right up to showtime. So I made the window shrink as the show gets closer, and it always ends at least 4 hours before the show.

    The small rules that make the window feel fair

    Hard cut-off: the deadline is whichever comes first: the window ending, or 4 hrs before the show. A cinema can push this to 6 hrs if it wants.

    Quiet hours: no deadline ever falls between 11 PM and 8 AM. It moves to 9 AM, unless that would break the 4-hr cut-off.

    Reminders: BookMyShow sends them, not Rohan: at the halfway point, 1 hr before, and 15 min before the deadline.

    Last call: when the deadline hits, Rohan gets 30 minutes to pay, hand the seat to someone else, or let it go. Only then is it released.

    Paying through WhatsApp: what the rules allow

    Before designing the payment, I read the actual NPCI, Meta and RBI rules. Each one ended up shaping a decision.

    What the rule says

    What I did about it

    NPCI · from 1 Oct 2025

    People can no longer send "request money" (collect) to each other on UPI.

    →

    I made friends pay BookMyShow directly, as a merchant. Nobody ever pays Rohan.

    NPCI · merchant collect phasing out

    Collect requests are on their way out for merchants on Android too.

    →

    I used UPI intent (opens the friend's UPI app) on phones and a QR on desktop.

    Meta · opt-in rule

    A business can only message someone first if they've opted in, with an approved template.

    →

    I let Rohan share links from their own WhatsApp. BookMyShow only messages friends who opted in, with SMS as backup.

    the show crosses 80% sold while a pass is open?

    Who gains, and why it's good business

    In the end, this isn't only a fix for Rohan. When the person booking carries less risk, more people are willing to organise, and more groups actually make it to the cinema.

    Friends

    BookMyShow

    Bulk

    group users coming in

    Bigger bookings, more seats per transaction, and every friend who opens a link becomes a new verified user. No rival offers this today.

    Cinemas

    4 hrs

    of protection before the show

    Unpaid seats come back with time to sell to walk-ins and other apps, and kept reserves are shared with the cinema, so a held seat is never a pure loss.

    The 4-hour cut-off is the cinema's safety net. Any seat a friend doesn't pay for goes back on sale with enough time to sell, and the reserve Rohan forfeits is split with the cinema as part of its revenue share. A held seat either sells, or it's paid for.

    Friends

    Share of links paid inside the window, time to pay, and how often people tap "Can't come"

    Guardrails

    Share of reserved seats released, resale of released seats, occupancy vs similar shows, abuse flags, support tickets

    How I'd roll it out

    I'd start with the quick wins from my matrix, test the riskier ideas in a small pilot, and only then scale.

    V1 · Split & Flex

    Quick wins

    WhatsApp share + pay page, dashboard, per-seat tickets, assign seats, smart seating, pay in full with "Pay Rohan back" links, cancel one seat, can't come / swap, an honest sell-out signal.

    Why now: It doesn't change how cinemas hold seats, and it proves friends will actually pay through a link.

    V2 · Group Pass pilot

    Big bites, with guardrails

    The reserve, pay windows with a cut-off, auto-release, and paying inside WhatsApp. One cinema chain, two cities, around colleges.

    Why now: It's the highest impact and the highest risk, so I'd test it with one partner first.

    V3 · Scale

    Later

    The show-time Lobby and UPI Reserve Pay (block the money, don't charge it), across all chains.

    Why now: It needs wider bank support and real data from the pilot.

    What I left out on purpose: a pay-now discount, friend-to-friend UPI collect, the old wallet split, and a flat ₹25 deposit.

    What I assumed, and how I'd check it

    I didn't have access to BookMyShow's internal data, so I backed each number with the closest public evidence I could find, and I've noted how I'd test it.

    1

    I assumed a group means 3–10 seats because the friend groups I interviewed were 5–8 people, and BookMyShow averages 2.6 tickets per booking. I’ll check it via the share of 3+ seat bookings.

    2

    I assumed a 25% reserve is fair because BookMyShow already charges 25% to cancel 2+ hrs before the show. I’ll check it via a pilot testing 25% vs 50%.

    3

    I assumed busy shows need 50% because BookMyShow’s late tier and IRCTC’s last-12-hr fee are both 50%. I’ll check it via abuse flags on hot shows.

    4

    I assumed 4 hrs is the right cut-off because it mirrors IRCTC’s chart time and stays before BookMyShow’s costlier 2-hr tier. I’ll check it via the resale rate of released seats.

    5

    I assumed 24 h / 12 h / 3 h / 45 min windows work because they scale down IndiGo’s 48–72 hr fare holds. I’ll check it via how long links actually take to get paid.

    6

    I assumed there should be no Group Pass within 5 hrs because IndiGo and IRCTC both stop holds close to departure. I’ll check it via pass demand by lead time.

    7

    I assumed Rohan should share the links because Meta needs opt-in before a business messages someone first. I’ll check it via link open rate by channel.

    8

    I assumed friends will pay by UPI intent or QR because NPCI ended person-to-person collect in Oct 2025. I’ll check it via payment success rate by method.

    9

    I assumed refunds take 5–7 days, or are instant as credit because that’s how BookMyShow refunds today, and RBI sets no fixed time. I’ll check it via refund complaints.

    10

    I assumed asking friends for money is the real pain because 3 of my 5 interviewees said so. I’ll check it via a 30–50 person survey.

    11

    I assumed cinemas will accept capped reserves because it matches the cancellation rules they already live with. I’ll check it via talks with cinema partners before the pilot.

    What I learned

    1

    The core idea lands. Both testers finished every task, and “paying only for my seat” was everyone’s favourite part.

    2

    The reserve is the weak spot. Testers say they understand it, but neither knew it is refunded, and trust in paying it was mixed.

    3

    The 6-hr release window felt right to both testers.

    4

    Paying for a friend was the least easy flow, and the reminder flow caused the only stuck moment.

    5

    The countdown and “seats fill next to the group” didn’t stand out for P2.

    What I’d change next

    1

    Say “refunded to you when they pay” right next to the ₹84.01 amount on the Pay screen and on the ticket, not only inside the collapsible card.

    2

    Show the refund as a status on the tracker, e.g. “₹84.01 back to you · Riya paid”.

    3

    Make “Pay for a friend” easier to find on the group screen.

    4

    Make the remind action more obvious when under 1 hr is left.

    5

    Test with 5–8 more people, including friends who only get the WhatsApp link.

    The idea works for testers. The money explanation doesn’t yet: the reserve has to read as “held, then refunded”, not as a fee.
