    It's a guarantee, not a payment for a friend: it comes back the moment that friend pays.

    closer to the show

    Reserve on every unpaid seat: 25% of the base price. On busy shows (60%+ sold, or an opening weekend) it's 50%, and the window is halved.

    The window shrinks as the show gets closer: 24 hrs → 12 hrs → 3 hrs → 45 min, borrowed from IndiGo and IRCTC holds.

    A hard cut-off before the show so the cinema can still resell unpaid seats.

    Too close to the show, Group Pass is off; a normal booking still works.

    Rules that shaped the design

    NPCI ended UPI collect requests (Oct 2025) → friends pay BookMyShow directly with UPI intent or QR. Nobody ever pays me.

    Meta needs opt-in before a business messages first → BookMyShow messages friends only after I consent at checkout.

    RBI sets no fixed refund time → reserves come back instantly as credit, or in 5–7 days to the bank.

    CCPA dark-pattern rules → no fake urgency and no pre-ticked add-ons.

    Guardrails, so it can't be misused

    Seat hoarding: 3–10 seats only, and a real 25% reserve instead of a flat ₹25.

    Holding seats for free: every unpaid seat costs its reserve, so fake friends aren't worth it.

    Late releases: unpaid seats go back on sale hours before the show, never at the last minute.

    Group fights: each paid seat is its own ticket, with its own cancellation.

    Cinemas

    4 hrs

    of protection before the show

    Unpaid seats come back with time to sell to walk-ins and other apps, and kept reserves are shared with the cinema, so a held seat is never a pure loss.

    The 4-hour cut-off is the cinema's safety net. Any seat a friend doesn't pay for goes back on sale with enough time to sell, and the reserve Rohan forfeits is split with the cinema as part of its revenue share. A held seat either sells, or it's paid for.

    How I'd roll it out

    V1 · Split & Flex

    Quick wins

    WhatsApp share + pay page, dashboard, per-seat tickets, assign seats, smart seating, pay in full with "Pay Rohan back" links, cancel one seat, can't come / swap, an honest sell-out signal.

    Why now: It doesn't change how cinemas hold seats, and it proves friends will actually pay through a link.

    V2 · Group Pass pilot

    Big bites, with guardrails

    The reserve, pay windows with a cut-off, auto-release, and paying inside WhatsApp. One cinema chain, two cities, around colleges.

    Why now: It's the highest impact and the highest risk, so I'd test it with one partner first.

    V3 · Scale

    Later

    The show-time Lobby and UPI Reserve Pay (block the money, don't charge it), across all chains.

    Why now: It needs wider bank support and real data from the pilot.

    What I assumed, and how I'd check it

    1

    I assumed a group means 3–10 seats because the friend groups I interviewed were 5–8 people, and BookMyShow averages 2.6 tickets per booking. I’ll check it via the share of 3+ seat bookings.

    2

    3

    4

    5

    I assumed 24 h / 12 h / 3 h / 45 min windows work because they scale down IndiGo’s 48–72 hr fare holds. I’ll check it via how long links actually take to get paid.

    6

    What I learned

    1

    The core idea lands. Both testers finished every task, and “paying only for my seat” was everyone’s favourite part.

    2

    The reserve is the weak spot. Testers say they understand it, but neither knew it is refunded, and trust in paying it was mixed.

    3

    The 6-hr release window felt right to both testers.

    4

    Paying for a friend was the least easy flow, and the reminder flow caused the only stuck moment.

    5

    The countdown and “seats fill next to the group” didn’t stand out for P2.

    What I’d change next

    1

    Say “refunded to you when they pay” right next to the ₹84.01 amount on the Pay screen and on the ticket, not only inside the collapsible card.

    2

    Show the refund as a status on the tracker, e.g. “₹84.01 back to you · Riya paid”.

    3

    Make “Pay for a friend” easier to find on the group screen.

    4

    Make the remind action more obvious when under 1 hr is left.

    5

    Test with 5–8 more people, including friends who only get the WhatsApp link.

    The idea works for testers. The money explanation doesn’t yet: the reserve has to read as “held, then refunded”, not as a fee.

    Why it's worth fixing

    It starts at 3+ seats. Two friends just settle one amount; bigger groups are where it breaks.

    It's a revenue leak, not just a complaint. BookMyShow earns per seat, so every group that shrinks or drops out is lost money for BookMyShow and the cinema.
